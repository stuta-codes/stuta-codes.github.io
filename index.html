Our story

Rooted in a family trade

Dhaago is the Nepali word for thread. Our family has worked with fabric for more than 20 years, and the supplier relationships we have built in Nepal are the heart of this online store.

We started Dhaago to make fashionable clothing, quality fabrics and personal tailoring easier to find, wherever you live in the United States.
